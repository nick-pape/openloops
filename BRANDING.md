## Key Concepts

### Open Loop
An open loop is any commitment, task, or capture that hasn't been completed or filed away. Open Loops helps you:
1. **Capture** all your open loops (notes, tasks, ideas)
2. **Organize** them into projects
3. **Plan** when to close them
4. **Track** progress until they're done

### The Philosophy
The app is built on the "Getting Things Done" concept that keeping open loops in your head creates mental overhead. By capturing everything externally and planning systematically, you free your mind to focus on execution.

## Product Description

**Short (Twitter bio):**
Open Loops: Local-first app for capturing notes, planning your day, and closing your open loops. Built with Electron + SQLite. Works offline, AI-optional.

**Medium (GitHub):**
Open Loops is a local-first desktop app for project notes and day planning. Capture text, images, and audio. Import your calendar and let AI help you plan. Everything stored locally. Works with Ollama for private AI features. Built for Windows, Mac, and Linux.

**Long (Website):**
Open Loops helps you capture everything, plan your day, and close your open loops.

**Capture freely**: Quickly save text notes, drop in images, or record voice memos. Everything organized by project.

**Plan intelligently**: Import your calendar and tasks. Generate smart time-blocked schedules that fit around your meetings.

**Stay local**: All your data stays on your machine. No cloud sync, no subscriptions, no privacy concerns. Optional AI features work with local Ollama or OpenAI.

**Built for power users**: Keyboard shortcuts, fast capture, offline-first. Electron app for Windows, Mac, and Linux.

## Use Cases

2. **Researchers**: Literature notes, interview transcripts, research planning
3. **Writers**: Article ideas, research clips, writing schedules
4. **Consultants**: Client notes, project documentation, time blocking

## Competitive Position

**vs. Notion/Obsidian**: Local-first, faster, no markdown required, integrated planning
**vs. Todoist/Things**: Richer capture (images/audio), project context, AI summaries
**vs. Reclaim/Motion**: No calendar OAuth required, local data, one-time purchase
**vs. Voice memos**: Automatic transcription, project organization, task integration
